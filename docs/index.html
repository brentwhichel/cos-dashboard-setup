Set up your own Chief of Staff Email & Teams Dashboard

Enter your priorities, people and accounts once. This page turns them into the exact prompts that build a private dashboard in your own Cowork: every email and Teams message that needs you, screened against your Chief of Staff constitution, researched, and drafted in your voice. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

What you get

  • A private dashboard app in Cowork with My tasks, Latest check and Last refreshed.
  • Every item shows priority, why it matters, a summary, who is on it (outsiders flagged), See original, private research notes and a draft in your voice.
  • Every customer email and Teams message, every @mention and every unread 1:1 chat lands on the dashboard.
  • Send, Reply all, add people, Mark complete, Reopen and Delete, each with undo.

What you need

  • Microsoft 365 Copilot with Cowork, including automations (event triggers and scheduled tasks) and App Builder.
  • Your own Outlook, Teams and OneDrive.
  • Your top priorities, your key people and your customer accounts with their email domains.
  • About 45 minutes for Steps 1 to 8, plus 15 minutes after the first week.

Field-tested. This is the same setup that has run in a live deployment since October 2026: 1,300+ emails screened, 270+ messages surfaced with researched drafts, and 50 replies sent from the dashboard, each one reviewed first.

    Lessons from the live build
    
    
    Know these before you start
    
    
      
      
    • !Editing pauses automations. After any change, re-activate the automation in Cowork Automations. The dashboard warns you when no check has landed for two business hours.
      • 
      
    • !Some external replies need Outlook. Your organization may block apps from emailing people outside the company. If Send is blocked, the draft is unchanged: send that reply from Outlook.
      • 
      
    • !Check the reply audience. A reply to a forwarded invite or an alert mailbox can go to the wrong person. When the summary says so, reply in Teams instead.
      • 
      
    • !Save files directly. A run that saves by downloading and re-uploading waits for an approval nobody sees. These instructions always edit files in place.
      • 
      
    • !Keep one feed. If OneDrive shows “conflicted” copies of comms-feed.json, ask Cowork to fold anything missing back into the main file, then delete the copies.
      • 
      
    • !Watch your credits. Every run uses Copilot Credits, and most runs are email screenings that end in a skip. Outlook rules that keep newsletters out of your Inbox, or an hourly Teams sweep, cut runs the most.
